Sub-processors

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Storecops processes personal data on behalf of the merchants who install it. This page lists every third party that can process that data, so a merchant can satisfy their own obligations under Article 28 of the GDPR and equivalent laws. It complements the Privacy Policy, which explains what we collect and why.

A Data Processing Agreement is available on request. Email privacy@storecops.com and we will send one for signature. We will also give advance notice of any change to this page — see Changes.

1. Sub-processors

These providers process merchant or shopper personal data on our behalf:

ProviderPurposeData involved
Railway Application hosting, persistent database volume, backups and log retention. All data the platform stores, including merchant and shopper personal data.
Shopify Source of store, product, order and customer data; app embedding; billing; mandatory privacy webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact). Store domain, product catalogue, orders, customer name/email/phone, access tokens (encrypted at rest).
Meta (WhatsApp Business API) Delivery of cart-recovery and campaign messages over WhatsApp; public Ad Library reads for competitor intelligence. Shopper phone number and message content, where the merchant has connected WhatsApp.
Resend Transactional and marketing email delivery. Shopper and merchant email address, message content.
BigCommerce Merchant platform integration (catalogue, orders) when the merchant connects BigCommerce. Store domain, product catalogue, orders, access tokens (encrypted at rest).
SerpApi Search-result data used for SEO recommendations. Search keywords only — no shopper or merchant personal data is sent.
Stripe Subscription payment processing, when enabled. Merchant billing contact and payment method, held by the provider.
Razorpay Subscription payment processing, when enabled. Merchant billing contact and payment method, held by the provider.
Redis Queue and cache backend, when the deployment configures it instead of in-process storage. Queued job payloads and session/throttle counters — may contain personal data.

2. Third-party assets loaded in your browser

These are not sub-processors: we do not send them personal data. But your browser requests files from them directly, so your IP address and the referring page reach them. They are listed for completeness.

ProviderWhat is loaded
unpkg (Lucide icon bundle) Icon set, loaded by the app and marketing pages.
jsDelivr Content delivery, permitted by the site Content-Security-Policy.
Google Fonts Web fonts used by the marketing and app pages.

3. Public data sources

For competitor and demand intelligence the platform reads publicly available pages. No personal data is sent, and no account relationship exists with these sites.

SourceWhat is read
Google Trends Daily trending searches, used as a demand signal.
Reddit Public subreddit listings, used as a demand signal.
Pinterest Public search pages, used as a demand signal.

4. Changes

This register is generated from the application's own configuration, so it is updated as part of the release that adds or removes a provider rather than after the fact. We will notify merchants by email before a new sub-processor begins processing personal data, giving an opportunity to object.

5. Your own obligations

If you are a merchant, you are the controller and we are your processor. You are responsible for the lawfulness of the data you ask us to process, for obtaining any consent your jurisdiction requires, and for your own disclosures to your shoppers. Storecops blocks marketing events until consent is recorded, but analytics events are permitted by default — see the Terms for the consent configuration you may need.
