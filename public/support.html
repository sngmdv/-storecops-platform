Response times

We aim to reply to every message within the windows below.

  • General support — within 24 hours · 7 days a week
  • Billing & subscription — within 12 hours · weekdays
  • Scale plan priority support — within 4 hours · weekdays
  • Critical / data-security incidents — within 1 hour · 24/7

Common topics

  • Privacy Policy — how we collect, use and protect your data
  • Terms of Service — platform usage, billing and liability
  • GDPR / data deletion — use the GDPR tools inside the app (Settings → Customer data) or email us
  • Unsubscribe — every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link
  • API documentation — the full REST API is available at /api/v1 with your API key

Report a security concern

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in Storecops, please email support@storecops.com with the subject line "Security concern". Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until we have had a chance to investigate and remediate. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will acknowledge your contribution.
