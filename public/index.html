About Storecops

Built by operators,

Storecops started with a simple observation: independent merchants don’t lose to big brands — they lose to blind spots. Carts nobody nudges. Stockouts nobody predicts. Price drops nobody matches in time. We build the team that never sleeps, so a one-person store can operate with the precision of a retail giant.

Today the platform runs a six-layer intelligence engine across 63 tools — and stays deliberately merchant-first: transparent attribution, data you own and can export anytime, and automation that always explains why it acted.
